# what-is-behind-
## GAME STORY

This is a horror game where there is a scary child in the house.

There was a family living in Blackwoods. The family had many secrets.

One day the child and the family went to the basement and they never came back.

When the Sheriff and police came to check the house they felt a strange presence inside the house.

They found out that the child did not die and his soul is still inside the house.

Now you have to survive in the house and exorcise the soul.

## YOUR MISSION

You have to collect 3 EYES OF EXORCISM.

After collecting all 3 eyes you have to take them to the shrine in the backyard.

Put all 3 eyes in the shrine to exorcise the soul and win the game.

But be careful because the scary child can catch you and kill you.

## LOCATIONS

### MAGICAL CUBE KEY

First you need to collect the MAGICAL CUBE KEY.

It is a magical yellow box key which opens the backyard.

You can find it on the dining table in the kitchen.

### FIRST EYE

The first EYE OF EXORCISM is in the garage near the car.

### SECOND EYE

The second EYE OF EXORCISM is placed on the altar table.

### THIRD EYE

The third EYE OF EXORCISM is on the top floor.

## HOW TO WIN

Collect the MAGICAL CUBE KEY.

Open the backyard.

Find all 3 EYES OF EXORCISM.

Go to the shrine in the backyard.

Put all 3 eyes in the shrine.

Exorcise the soul and win the game.

## CONTROLS

W = Move Forward

S = Move Back

A = Move Right

D = Move Left

## WARNING

The child is inside the house.

He can find you and kill you.

Stay careful and try to survive while finding the eyes.
